# Explore Cities - Travel & Tourism App

A mobile-first tourism discovery app that helps users explore cities, find attractions, and plan trips. 
Designed for travelers looking for recommendations on places, accommodations, and adventures.

## 🎯 Project Purpose

The purpose of Explore Cities is to make travel planning simple by providing:
1. **City Exploration** - Discover popular destinations and attractions in any city
2. **Personalized Recommendations** - Curated list of must-visit places with ratings
3. **Category-based Search** - Quickly filter by Accommodation, Adventure, Beaches, etc.
4. **Local Insights** - Help tourists find historical sites, natural spots, and experiences

Perfect for tourism boards, travel agencies, or as a standalone travel guide app.

## ✨ Key Features

### **1. Home & Discovery**
- **Banner**: "Explore Cities! Enjoy your tour!" with scenic background
- **Search Bar**: Search by name, city, or category
- **Notifications**: Bell icon with unread count

### **2. Categories**
Quick filters for different travel types:
- **Accommodation** - Hotels, resorts, stays
- **Adventure** - Hiking, tours, activities  
- **Beaches** - Coastal destinations and water activities
- More categories available via "View All"

### **2. Destination Cards**
Each destination shows:
- **High-quality image** of the place
- **Name**: e.g. Karimabad Hunza, K2, Clifton Beach, French Beach
- **Rating**: Star rating out of 5.0. e.g. 4.4 ★
- **Location**: e.g. Hunza Valley, Karakoram Range
- **Bookmark Icon**: Save to favorites

### **3. Recommendations**
Curated places with:
- High-quality images
- Place name
- Star rating system. Ex: `Badshahi Mosque - 4.4 ⭐`
- Examples shown: `Badshahi Mosque`, `Mohenjo-Daro`

# Place Detail Screen - Explore Cities App

A detailed view page for each tourist destination in the Explore Cities app. 
Shows full information, location, and map for a selected place.

## 🎯 Purpose of This Screen
To give users complete information about a single attraction so they can decide to visit, 
save it, or navigate to it. This screen is the core of the "discovery to action" flow.

### **"Other Attractions" Section**
Vertical scrollable list of destinations with:
- **Thumbnail Image** - High quality photo of each place
- **Name**: Badshahi Mosque, Mohenjo-Daro, Faisal Mosque, Shalimar Gardens
- **Short Description**: 1-2 line summary. Ex: "One of the largest mosques in the world..."
- **Location Tag**: Lahore, Larkana, Islamabad
- **Rating**: 4.4 ⭐ for each attraction
